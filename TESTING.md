# Testing Imprint Plays

> For: Brad, Ev, Andy (and whoever else gets Imprint access)
> Status: Beta — actively being refined based on playtests

---

## What it is

A set of 10 games that run inside Imprint. Not personality quizzes, not therapy. The point is the weird moment where something real comes out of something that felt like a game. Think of it as chaos goblin energy with a vault underneath.

---

## How to start

In any Imprint session, just say one of:

- **"Imprint Plays"**
- **"let's play"**
- **"game time"**

Imprint will load the game menu and you pick from there.

---

## The 10 games

| # | Game | What it is |
|---|------|------------|
| 1 | Word Association | Back and forth words, no rules — Imprint reacts to the weird ones |
| 2 | Two Truths & a Lie | You give 3, Imprint guesses the lie using your vault |
| 3 | Would You Rather | Imprint picks tough either/ors from your actual life context |
| 4 | Rank These | 5 things, you rank them, the order tells a story |
| 5 | Am I The Asshole? | Imprint presents an ambiguous scenario, you judge it |
| 6 | Desert Island | 5 items, you pick 3, you explain why — the why is the game |
| 7 | Story Chain | One sentence each, back and forth, no plan |
| 8 | Guess What Imprint Thinks | Imprint makes a confident (often wrong) guess about you — the correction is the fun part |
| 9 | Young [Name] | Imprint describes a scenario for you at age 8–12, you answer as that kid |
| 10 | Mad Libs | Collaborative chaos — Imprint fills a template using your vault |

---

## What to test for

- Does it feel like a **game** or like homework?
- Does anything surprising come out of it?
- Does the game create a moment where Imprint goes "okay wtf did you mean by *that*"?
- Does it feel like **Imprint** or like a generic chatbot?

The standard: chaos goblin energy. If it feels like a therapy question or an interrogation, flag it.

---

## What we know works (from internal playtests)

- **Word Association** — pit stop after 10 rounds, banter about the weird ones. Do NOT debrief during the game.
- **Story Chain** — one sentence each. If they write two, call it out. Debrief ONLY after.
- **Am I The Asshole?** — no Reddit jargon. Natural language verdicts only. The point isn't the verdict.
- **Guess What Imprint Thinks** — confident and slightly wrong is better than accurate and hedged. No profile = pure chaos mode = equally fun.
- **Young [Name]** — ordinary moments, not big dramatic events. The kid's voice is the reveal.
- **Mad Libs** — 5 minutes max. Sorbet game. Don't overthink it.

## What we retired (and why)

- **Rapid Fire** — interrogation energy. Yes/no leaves no room for stories or chaos goblin moments.
- **Finish the Sentence** — felt like therapy prompts.
- **20 Questions** — car game, takes work, no organic chaos moments.

## How to give feedback

Drop notes in Slack or just tell Jay. Key things to flag:
- Any game that felt off (too slow, too serious, too much like work)
- Any moment that worked really well
- Anything that felt weird without a profile loaded (Imprint should still work, just with more chaos)
